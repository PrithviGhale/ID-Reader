# AgeCheck 1.7.0 - A new counter desk

- Rebuilt the interface around a top navigation bar, a clearer scan sequence, and a more prominent age and expiration panel. Smaller windows use the menu.
- Added four saved color themes in Settings: Midnight lime, Paper, Ocean, and Sand.
- Added a short pulse while a barcode is received and checked. Motion remains optional and stops when the result appears.
- Highlighted the calendar days left before expiration or the days since expiration on completed checks.
- Removed the costly per-button blur effect and adjusted narrow-window headings to avoid clipping.

AgeCheck calculates age and expiration from barcode fields. It does not authenticate the physical ID or replace a comparison of the card, photograph and customer.
